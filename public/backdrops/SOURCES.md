# Gym backdrop photos

All three are free to use, change and publish under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license); credit isn't required, but it's listed here. Each was downloaded at 2400px wide, turned black and white, cropped to a 2400×1000 band around its subject (the dumbbell photo to 2376 wide, to drop a light edge) and saved as WebP under 250 KB.
