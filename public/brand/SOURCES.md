# Sign-in button logos

- `google-g.png`: Google's [Sign in with Google branding asset](https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png). Follow the [Google sign-in branding guidelines](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines/).
- `apple.svg`: Apple logo from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/apple.svg). Follow the [Sign in with Apple design guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sign-in-with-apple/).
