Acceptance Test 1: Today's exercises appear in order with weight and reps boxes

Acceptance Test 1: Today's exercises appear in order with weight and reps boxes

Before you start: Connect to the UB VPN, or use the campus network. If you are logged in, log out. Open https://cattle.cse.buffalo.edu/CSE442/2026-Fall/cse-442y/#/login, type marcus.malone@example.com and Marcus-Bench225!, and press "Log in". The Dashboard should open.

Step 1: Open https://cattle.cse.buffalo.edu/CSE442/2026-Fall/cse-442y/#/dev/workout-log.

Step 2: Look at the top of the page. You should see the words "TODAY'S WORKOUT", the heading "Push", and the line "Push Pull Legs · Chest, shoulders, triceps · About 45 min".

Step 3: Look at the list under it. You should see three exercises, from top to bottom, in this order: "Bench press", "Overhead press", "Triceps pushdown". Each one shows "0 sets".

Step 4: Look inside each exercise. You should see "Set 1" with a box labelled "Weight (lb)", a box labelled "Reps", and a "Save set" button.

Step 5: Look at the very bottom of the list. You should see one "Complete Workout" button.

Test passes if: All three exercises appear in the order above, each with a "Weight (lb)" box, a "Reps" box and a "Save set" button, and one "Complete Workout" button sits below them.

Test fails if: Any of these happen:

- an exercise is missing or the order is different;

- an exercise has no weight box or no reps box;

- there is no "Complete Workout" button, or one for each exercise instead of one at the bottom.

Acceptance Test 2: Logging a set for each exercise

Acceptance Test 2: Logging a set for each exercise

Before you start: Log in as in Test 1 and open https://cattle.cse.buffalo.edu/CSE442/2026-Fall/cse-442y/#/dev/workout-log.

Step 1: Under "Bench press", click the "Weight (lb)" box and type 135. Click the "Reps" box and type 8. Press "Save set".

Step 2: Look under "Bench press". You should see "Set 1" with "135 lb × 8" next to it, a green "Set 1 saved", and the count change to "1 set". A new "Set 2" form should appear below it, with the 135 and 8 shown as faint grey hints.

Step 3: In the "Set 2" form, type 135 and 6, then press "Save set". You should see "Set 2" listed under "Set 1", and the count change to "2 sets".

Step 4: Under "Overhead press", type 65 in "Weight (lb)" and 10 in "Reps", then press "Save set". You should see "Set 1" with "65 lb × 10".

Step 5: Under "Triceps pushdown", type 0 in "Weight (lb)" and 12 in "Reps", then press "Save set". You should see "Set 1" listed (0 means a bodyweight set).

Step 6: Check that the sets stay under the exercise they were typed into, and that the page did not flash white and reload.

Test passes if: Each saved set is listed under its own exercise, in the order it was saved, with the right numbers, and the exercise's count goes up each time.

Test fails if: A set is missing, appears under the wrong exercise, shows different numbers from the ones typed, or the page reloads.

Acceptance Test 3: Empty or invalid weight and reps are refused

Acceptance Test 3: Empty or invalid weight and reps are refused

Before you start: Log in as in Test 1 and open https://cattle.cse.buffalo.edu/CSE442/2026-Fall/cse-442y/#/dev/workout-log.

Step 1: Under "Bench press", leave both boxes empty and press "Save set".

Step 2: Look under "Bench press". You should see "Enter a weight (0 for bodyweight)." in red under "Weight (lb)" and "Enter your reps." in red under "Reps". The count should still say "0 sets".

Step 3: Type 135 in "Weight (lb)" and 0 in "Reps", then press "Save set". You should see "Reps must be between 1 and 100." and "0 sets".

Step 4: Type 1501 in "Weight (lb)" and 8.5 in "Reps", then press "Save set". You should see "Weight must be between 0 and 1500 lb." and "Reps must be a whole number."

Step 5: Type 135.555 in "Weight (lb)" and 8 in "Reps", then press "Save set". You should see "Use at most 2 decimal places, like 137.25."

Step 6: Type 137.25 in "Weight (lb)" and 8 in "Reps", then press "Save set". You should see "Set 1" with "137.25 lb × 8" and the count change to "1 set".

Test passes if: Each wrong value shows its exact red message and nothing is saved, and the correct values in step 6 are saved.

Test fails if: A wrong value is saved, a message is missing or worded differently, or the correct values in step 6 are refused.

Acceptance Test 4: Complete Workout needs at least one logged set

Acceptance Test 4: Complete Workout needs at least one logged set

Before you start: Log in as in Test 1 and open https://cattle.cse.buffalo.edu/CSE442/2026-Fall/cse-442y/#/dev/workout-log. Do not log any sets.

Step 1: Press the "Complete Workout" button.

Step 2: Look under the button. You should see "Log at least one set before completing." in red, and every exercise should still show "0 sets".

Step 3: Under "Bench press", type 135 in "Weight (lb)" and 8 in "Reps", but do not press "Save set". Press "Complete Workout" again.

Step 4: Look under the button. You should see "Save or clear the set you're still editing before completing."

Step 5: Check that the "Complete Workout" button is still there and the boxes are still editable.

Test passes if: Both exact red messages appear, and the workout is not completed.

Test fails if: "Workout completed" appears, or a message is missing or worded differently.

Acceptance Test 5: Completing the workout locks the logged sets

Acceptance Test 5: Completing the workout locks the logged sets

Before you start: Log in as in Test 1 and open https://cattle.cse.buffalo.edu/CSE442/2026-Fall/cse-442y/#/dev/workout-log.

Step 1: Under "Bench press", type 135 and 8 and press "Save set". Under "Overhead press", type 65 and 10 and press "Save set". Leave "Triceps pushdown" empty.

Step 2: Press the "Complete Workout" button once.

Step 3: Look at the bottom of the page. The button should be replaced by "Workout completed".

Step 4: Look under "Bench press" and "Overhead press". You should see "Set 1" with "135 lb × 8" and "Set 1" with "65 lb × 10", the words "Workout completed — these sets can no longer be changed.", and no "Weight (lb)" box, "Reps" box or "Save set" button.

Step 5: Look under "Triceps pushdown". You should see "No sets logged." and no boxes.

Test passes if: "Workout completed" replaces the button, the saved sets stay on screen, and every box and "Save set" button is gone.

Test fails if: The button is still there, a saved set disappears or changes, or you can still type in a weight or reps box.

Acceptance Test 6: Logging fails clearly when the connection drops

Acceptance Test 6: Logging fails clearly when the connection drops

Before you start: Log in as in Test 1. Open https://cattle.cse.buffalo.edu/CSE442/2026-Fall/cse-442y/#/dev/workout-log?simulate=offline (this makes every save fail, as if the connection dropped).

Step 1: Under "Bench press", type 135 and 8 and press "Save set".

Step 2: Look under "Bench press". You should see a red error message and a "Retry" button. The count should still say "0 sets", and the 135 and 8 should still be in the boxes.

Step 3: Press the "Complete Workout" button. You should see "Log at least one set before completing.", because nothing was saved.

Step 4: Remove "?simulate=offline" from the address and press Enter. Press "Retry". You should see "Set 1" with "135 lb × 8" and the count change to "1 set".

Test passes if: The failed save shows an error without losing what you typed, and "Retry" saves it once the connection is back.

Test fails if: The set shows as saved while offline, the typed numbers disappear, or "Retry" does not save it.
